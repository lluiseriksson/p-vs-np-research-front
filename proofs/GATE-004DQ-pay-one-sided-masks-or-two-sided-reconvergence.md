# GATE-004DQ — pay one-sided masks or two-sided defect reconvergence

**Label: EXPLORATORY**

LEMMA-224 splits every first code-`10` cancellation into a one-sided mask or a
two-changing-input interaction. NG-165 shows that cancellation existence by
itself is not a physical payment.

## Falsifiable theorem

For every nonzero cut defect arising in GATE-004DP, follow a named path to its
first zero-defect binary gate `k` and prove one of the following, globally
deduplicated across all defects:

1. **one-sided branch:** the unchanged mask input and changed input cone form
   a jointly replaceable region whose exact net saving supplies a disjoint
   host toward `D_b^DAG` or strictly decreases `W,Q,R_0`;
2. **two-sided branch:** the two changed inputs have named upstream defect
   paths whose reconvergence forces a previously uncharged satisfying loss,
   a non-bridge deletion, or destruction of a named cycle coordinate; or
3. the cancellation can be moved by an explicit size-preserving exchange to
   obtain a strict earlier-potential descent.

The Boolean masking identity alone, an undifferentiated cycle-rank count, and
terminal-output equality are insufficient. The theorem is falsified by a
refined minimum endpoint with positive aligned deficit in which every first
cancellation is physically shared, all exact joint replacements have zero net
saving, all cycle changes are contractions, and no potential descends.
