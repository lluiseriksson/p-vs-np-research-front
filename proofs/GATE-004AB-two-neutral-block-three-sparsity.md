# GATE-004AB — two translated neutral blocks suppress signed clauses through width three

**Label: PROVED**

## Falsifiable theorem

Use the universally neutral alphabet `N_L` defined in GATE-004AA. For fixed
`L` and `rho>L`, form a length-`4rho` slot family containing:

1. the ENC-020 coordinate-dense options and all-one option;
2. the tunable option `A_rho`; and
3. every exact context obtained by placing up to two nonoverlapping members
   of `N_L` at four-aligned positions, filling every gap with one bits.

LEMMA-071 proves this with `L=36` and `c_3=78`.

## Why two blocks are the next threshold

LEMMA-067 defeats one bounded block because it can introduce at most one zero
on a distant triple. Two independently placed blocks can realize patterns
with two prescribed zero coordinates, while the mostly-zero `A_rho` can
supply the all-zero pattern on most triples. This removes the immediate
four-missing-pattern count; it does not prove sparsity.

All added options remain exact neutral contexts by composition. For fixed
`L` and sufficiently large `rho`, their zero runs remain bounded by a
constant while `A_rho` retains the tunable run. Coordinate density and the
all-one option are inherited.

## First attack

Derive the exact local three-coordinate pattern alphabet generated by two
translated universally neutral blocks. Separate triples by coordinate gaps
and boundary type. Either prove all interior patterns occur and confine every
common signed triple to constant boundary regions, or extract a new distant
packing obstruction.

LEMMA-068 completes the distant-partition case using only the ten ENC-022
blocks: all but six canonical distant triples realize all eight patterns. The
ten-block alphabet still fails locally by LEMMA-069. LEMMA-071 completes the
repair with identifiers through 68: its finite translation certificate covers
all interior local and intermediate types, and the boundary plus `A_rho` one
bits form the claimed 78-coordinate hitting set.
