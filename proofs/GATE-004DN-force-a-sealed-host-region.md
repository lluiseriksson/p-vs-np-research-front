# GATE-004DN — force a sealed host region

**Label: EXPLORATORY**

LEMMA-220 gives the exact parent-preserving interface for a changed host
region. NG-162 shows that the first functional seal can lie at arbitrary
depth in nonminimal circuits.

## Falsifiable theorem

For every refined minimum endpoint with positive aligned circuit deficit,
construct disjoint host rewrites whose full forward changed regions terminate
at functionally sealed frontiers and whose net physical saving/repurposing
covers `D_b^DAG`; or prove that an unsealed forward region forces a named
counterflow transfer, third satisfying loss, non-bridge deletion, forbidden
cycle change, or strict `W,Q,R_0` descent.

Every changed gate up to the seal and every exit edge must be named. A bounded
consumer radius, terminal-output equality alone, and loss membership alone are
insufficient. The theorem is falsified by a refined minimum endpoint whose
candidate hosts generate unbounded unsealed changed regions with insufficient
joint replacement saving and no exact contradiction.
