# GATE-004DI — couple private deficit to global minimum resources

**Label: EXPLORATORY**

LEMMA-215 makes the joint saving in NG-157 exact, and NG-158 shows it remains
one while private deficit grows. No local interface or cofactor-region cost
examined so far forces payment of `D_b`.

## Falsifiable theorem

For every refined minimum endpoint with a counted boundary of deficit
`D_b>0`, prove that global minimum structure supplies a collection of
pairwise physically disjoint resources whose total is at least `D_b`:

1. minimum joint cofactor-circuit savings of jointly masked escape regions;
2. gates absent from named satisfying prunings beyond already charged carrier
   losses;
3. repurposable non-bridge or private gates; or
4. units of strict lexicographic descent obtained by an explicit same-size
   rewrite.

Shared gates and pruning losses must be charged once. The proof must derive
the collection from minimum size and the exact three two-gate satisfying-loss
budgets; selecting a convenient local region is insufficient. Alternatively,
prove that positive unpaid deficit forces an aligned boundary formula of
smaller cost, contradiction, or an impossible incomparable/raw-input branch.

The theorem is falsified by a refined minimum parent with positive unpaid
deficit after the union of all admissible joint savings, exact pruning losses,
private gates, and non-bridge resources is deduplicated, and with no strict
potential descent. No such parent is claimed or constructed.
