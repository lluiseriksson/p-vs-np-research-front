# Adversarial protocol for a possible full solution

If a candidate terminal proof appears, all unrelated expansion stops.

1. Freeze the exact commit and candidate claim.
2. Search every dependency for the terminal statement or an equivalent hidden
   assumption.
3. Re-run the relativization and algebrization tests and identify the exact
   nonrelativizing/nonalgebrizing step.
4. For a circuit route, audit constructivity, largeness, and usefulness against
   the natural-proofs framework.
5. Normalize every quantifier: one machine/exponent, all inputs, worst case;
   or one language, every circuit exponent, infinitely many failure lengths.
6. Audit uniformity, advice, gates, depth, fan-in, randomness, promises,
   distributions, oracles, and fields.
7. Re-prove every reduction with explicit length and circuit-size blow-up.
8. Compare every invoked theorem with its primary source and hypotheses.
9. Reproduce all checks from a cold clone with pinned toolchains.
10. Formalize stable critical implications where feasible and inspect axioms.
11. Seek independent adversarial review.
12. Prepare a manuscript only after the complete chain survives; until then the
    candidate remains `EXPLORATORY`.
