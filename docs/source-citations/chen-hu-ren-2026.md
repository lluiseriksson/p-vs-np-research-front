# CHR26 — new algebrization barriers

Lijie Chen, Yang Hu, and Hanlin Ren, “New Algebrization Barriers to Circuit
Lower Bounds via Communication Complexity of Missing-String,” ITCS 2026.

- DOI/proceedings: https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.37
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14038
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claim: the paper develops new algebrization barriers and describes
  the current general-circuit lower-bound frontier, including the absence of
  superpolynomial lower bounds for explicit NEXP functions against unrestricted
  general circuits.
- Scope control: its communication lower bounds and oracle constructions are
  barrier evidence, not terminal P-versus-NP results.
