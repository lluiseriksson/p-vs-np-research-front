# KLNSW93 — combinatorial characterization of read-once formulae

Mauricio Karchmer, Nathan Linial, Ilan Newman, Michael Saks, and Avi
Wigderson, “Combinatorial Characterization of Read-Once Formulae,”
*Discrete Mathematics* 114(1–3), 275–282 (1993).

- Primary DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(93)90372-Z
- Author-hosted primary PDF:
  https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/PUBLICATIONS/MYPAPERS/KLNSW93/paper.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-11
- Consumed context: monotone read-once functions are characterized by their
  minterms/maxterms and by a `P_4`-free co-occurrence graph; the paper also
  records the nonmonotone extension by complementing the fixed-polarity
  variables.
