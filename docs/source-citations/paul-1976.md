# PAUL76 — disjoint-variable realization need not be additive

Wolfgang J. Paul, “Realizing Boolean Functions on Disjoint Sets of
Variables,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 2(3), 383–396 (1976).

- Primary DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(76)90089-X
- Archival author/textbook-hosted scan:
  https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/Knizka/Paul-disjoint76.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-11
- Consumed claim: for every positive `epsilon`, Paul constructs arbitrarily
  complex scalar Boolean functions `f` for which the OR composition of two
  disjoint copies, `OR(f(x),f(y))`, has combinational complexity at most
  `(1+epsilon)C(f)`. The paper also states that circuits obtained from an
  evident disjoint decomposition need not be optimal.
- Exact source model: acyclic circuits of two-input/one-output switching
  gates, with size equal to the number of indegree-two gates. The gate set is
  not the repository's counted fan-in-two AND/OR plus counted unary NOT basis.
- Scope control: PAUL76 is not a counterexample to the repository's
  implication-tail identities, GATE-004BA, or any canonical SAT statement.
  Its functions and composition are different and its basis/size convention
  is richer. It rules out importing a basis-agnostic direct-sum intuition.

Design consequence: every base-tail additivity, resource-survival, or
decomposition-normal-form claim in this repository must be proved for the
exact AND/OR/NOT basis and the exact one-negative clause product. Disjoint
variable supports alone are not evidence.
