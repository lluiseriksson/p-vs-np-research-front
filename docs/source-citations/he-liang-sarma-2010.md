# HLS10 — limiting negations between formulas and general circuits

Jing He, Hongyu Liang, and Jayalal Sarma M. N., “Limiting Negations in
Bounded Treewidth and Upward Planar Circuits,” *MFCS 2010*, LNCS 6281,
417–428 (2010).

- Primary DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-15155-2_37
- Author-hosted primary PDF:
  https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~jayalal/research/papers/HLS10.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-11
- Consumed claim: bounded-treewidth circuits explicitly interpolate between
  formulas and unrestricted DAGs for inversion-complexity questions; the
  paper records `I(f)=ceil(log_2(d(f)+1))` for circuits and `I_F(f)=d(f)` for
  formulas and proves size/negation transformations in bounded treewidth.
- Scope control: these are topology-restricted results and transformation
  upper bounds. They do not prove the exact unrestricted AND/OR/NOT size of
  the repository's one-negative read-once clause product, an additive base
  theorem, or diagonal quotient survival.
