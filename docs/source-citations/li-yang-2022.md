# LY22 — 3.1n unrestricted-circuit lower bound

Jiatu Li and Tianqi Yang, “3.1n − o(n) Circuit Lower Bounds for Explicit
Functions,” STOC 2022.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3519935.3519976
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claim: an explicitly defined affine disperser has unrestricted
  Boolean circuit size at least `3.1n-o(n)`, improving the prior linear record.
- Scope control: the bound is linear, is for the paper's explicit affine
  disperser, and is not a superlinear or SAT lower bound.
