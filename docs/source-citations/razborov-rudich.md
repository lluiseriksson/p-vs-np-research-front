# RR97 — natural proofs

Alexander A. Razborov and Steven Rudich, “Natural Proofs,” *Journal of Computer
and System Sciences* 55(1), 24–35 (1997).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1006/jcss.1997.1494
- Author-accessible PDF: https://mit6875.github.io/PAPERS/natural_proofs.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claim: under strong pseudorandomness assumptions, constructive and
  large properties cannot be useful for superpolynomial general-circuit lower
  bounds of the targeted kind.
- Scope control: the barrier is conditional and applies to the defined
  natural-proof framework, not every conceivable proof.
