# CDJ26 — constructive gate elimination

Marco Carmosino, Ngu Dang, and Tim Jackman, “Constructive Separations from Gate
Elimination,” arXiv preprint (2026), together with “Convergent Gate Elimination
and Constructive Circuit Lower Bounds.”

- Constructive separations: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23958
- Convergent formalization: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17942
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claim: known gate-elimination arguments can be made constructive
  (refuters can find errors) and the demonstrated unrestricted-basis lower
  bounds remain linear.
- Scope control: these papers do not claim superlinear general-circuit lower
  bounds or a SAT separation; they inform the method audit only.
