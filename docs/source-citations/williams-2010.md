# WIL10 — improving exhaustive search

Ryan Williams, “Improving Exhaustive Search Implies Superpolynomial Lower
Bounds,” STOC 2010.

- Author PDF: https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/improved-algs-lbs2.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claim: Theorem 1.1 transfers a superpolynomial-factor improvement
  for general Circuit-SAT, for every polynomial input-circuit size, to
  `NEXP notsubseteq P/poly` under the theorem's deterministic/co-nondeterministic
  algorithm conditions.
- Scope control: the conclusion concerns NEXP and does not by itself imply
  `P!=NP` or a SAT circuit lower bound.
