# WIL11 — non-uniform ACC lower bounds

Ryan Williams, “Non-Uniform ACC Circuit Lower Bounds,” IEEE Conference on
Computational Complexity (2011), 115–125.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/CCC.2011.36
- Author PDF: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ryanw/acc-lbs-ccc.pdf
- Accessed: 2026-08-10
- Consumed claims: `NEXP notsubseteq ACC0`; and the strengthened
  class-specific SAT-algorithm-to-lower-bound theorem with explicit size/depth
  overhead and closure requirements.
- Scope control: ACC circuits have restricted constant depth and gate types;
  the result is not a lower bound against general P/poly circuits.
